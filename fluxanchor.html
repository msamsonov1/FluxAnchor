Electrodynamic-Magnetic Coordination for Regolith containing Nanophase Iron ($np\text{-}\mathrm{Fe}^0$)

Lunar regolith is not an inert dielectric dust. Space weathering, solar wind micrometeorite impacts, and vapor deposition embed billions of submicron metallic iron globules ($np\text{-}\mathrm{Fe}^0$) inside amorphous agglutinitic silicate glass, conferring a complex mixed electrostatic-paramagnetic impedance profile.

01

Coupled Lorentz & Dielectrophoretic Transport

The net force $\mathbf{F}_{\text{net}}$ acting upon an agglutinitic lunar grain with surface charge $Q$, radius $r_p$, and magnetic moment $\mathbf{m}_p$ subject to multi-phase travelling electrostatic $\mathbf{E}(t)$ and magnetic gradient fields $\mathbf{B}(t)$ is governed by:

$$\mathbf{F}_{\text{net}} = Q(\mathbf{E} + \mathbf{v}_p \times \mathbf{B}) + 2\pi \varepsilon_0 \varepsilon_m r_p^3 \operatorname{Re}[K(\omega)] \nabla |\mathbf{E}|^2 + (\mathbf{m}_p \cdot \nabla)\mathbf{B}$$

Where $K(\omega) = \frac{\varepsilon_p^* - \varepsilon_m^*}{\varepsilon_p^* + 2\varepsilon_m^*}$ is the Clausius-Mossotti factor governing negative/positive dielectrophoresis (DEP), and $(\mathbf{m}_p \cdot \nabla)\mathbf{B}$ constitutes the high-gradient magnetophoretic extraction vector.

02

Nanophase Iron ($np\text{-}\mathrm{Fe}^0$) Magnetization Dynamics

Submicron metallic iron globules ($4\text{ to }30\,\mathrm{nm}$) exhibit superparamagnetism transitioning into stable single-domain ferromagnetism at cryogenic lunar night temperatures ($T < 100\,\mathrm{K}$):

$$\mathbf{m}_p = V_{\text{Fe}} M_s \left[ \coth\left(\frac{\mu_0 m_0 B}{k_B T}\right) - \frac{k_B T}{\mu_0 m_0 B} \right] \hat{\mathbf{b}} + \frac{\chi_{\text{host}}}{\mu_0} V_p \mathbf{B}$$

The Smearing Catastrophe: If $|\mathbf{B}|$ exceeds the magnetic saturation threshold $B_{\text{sat}} \approx 0.18\,\mathrm{T}$ without synchronized phase sweeping ($\partial \phi / \partial t$), the magnetic gradient pins metallic agglutinates firmly against the glass, inducing optical abrasion and irreversible transmission loss.

03

Real-Time $(Q, \Phi)$ State Observer Inversion

Optical backscatter sensors and capacitive pick-off traces continually estimate the aggregate charge density $Q$ and inductive magnetic flux linkage perturbation $\delta \Phi$:

$$\mathcal{S}_{\text{coordination}} = \frac{\nabla \Phi(t) \cdot \mathbf{v}_{\text{drift}}(Q)}{\|\nabla \Phi\| \cdot \|\mathbf{v}_{\text{drift}}\|}, \quad \text{where } \mathbf{v}_{\text{drift}} = \mu_e \mathbf{E}_{\parallel} + \frac{Q}{6\pi \eta r_p} \mathbf{E}_{\perp}$$

When $\mathcal{S}_{\text{coordination}} < 0.62$, the embedded microcontroller executes a 4-phase spatial excitation shift $\Delta \theta_k = \frac{2\pi k}{4} + \Omega_{\text{rot}}(t)$, rotating magnetic field vortices to roll rather than drag the metallic inclusions off the optical path.
